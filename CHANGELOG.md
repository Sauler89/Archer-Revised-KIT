# Changelog

## v0.1.0-dev

- Initialized standalone Archer Revised project.
- Imported the EET-relevant Improved Archer technical resources under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Removed the Improved Archer -1 Constitution penalty.
- Added d8 Archer Hit Die progression.
- Enabled Grand Mastery with longbows, shortbows and crossbows.
- Removed Two-Weapon Style proficiency.
- Retained Rooting Shot, Power Shot, Explosive Shot and Conjure Elemental Ammunition as the initial technical base.
- Added explicit conflict checks for A7 Improved Archer and Artisan's Kitpack Archer Overhaul.
- Added English and Italian installer strings and revised kit descriptions.
- Temporarily disabled A7-specific custom HLAs pending the native Archer Revised HLA implementation.
- Added a native Rapid Shot toggle: +1 APR, -4 ranged THAC0, improving to -2 at level 12.
- Added a -3 melee THAC0 Archer drawback and an extra melee penalty while Rapid Shot is active to prevent melee APR abuse.
- Rapid Shot activation/deactivation and the level-12 upgrade are generated entirely by Archer Revised; no Artisan resources are redistributed.
